What is Ntivo EdTech?

Ntivo EdTech builds software for educators and schools. We focus on the everyday work around teaching — planning lessons, setting assessments, and organising teaching materials — and build tools that make that work easier and less repetitive.

Product Focus

Langa Workspace

A workspace for South African teachers working with CAPS. Plan your lessons, create your assessments, and keep your teaching documents organised together in one place.

How Langa helps you teach

Create Assessments

The reality of teaching.
